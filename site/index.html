How to play

Complete the chain. Each word joins the word below it to make a compound word or phrase.

  1. You get the top word, plus the first letter of every word below it.
  2. Work down one word at a time. You get one guess per word. Miss, and you move on without seeing the answer.
  3. Miss two in a row and it’s game over.
  4. Typos, made-up words and other real compounds don’t cost you anything. Just try again.
  5. You have 3 hints per puzzle. Each reveals one letter, but never a word’s last letter.
  6. Each solved word scores a point for every letter still hidden.
